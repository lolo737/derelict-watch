Report an empty building

Seen a house or shop that has sat empty on your street? Photograph it, pin it, and tell us how long it has been dark. It takes about a minute.

Before you take a photo

  • Buildings, not people. Wait until nobody is in the frame. Do not photograph number plates.
  • If someone or a car still sneaks in, use Blur an area to pixelate it before sending.
  • Stay on the public footpath. Never enter a property, and never photograph through windows.
  • Location data hidden in the photo file is removed on your phone before upload. Only the pin you place is shared.

    About Derelict Watch

    Derelict Watch is an independent community project. It is not run by, funded by, or affiliated with any government department, local authority or State body. Reports here are not official records and are not a complaint or a notice to any authority.

    Official vacancy surveys are slow and expensive. Neighbours already know which houses have been dark for years. This app lets them say so: a photo, a pin, and a rough idea of how long.

    Privacy

    • Reports are about buildings. Please never include people, faces, number plates or names.
    • Photos are shrunk and re-encoded on your phone. GPS, camera model and other hidden metadata in the original file are stripped before upload.
    • No account, no name, no email. The app does not use cookies or analytics.
    • Map tiles are loaded from OpenStreetMap, whose servers see your IP address like any website.
    • Anyone can flag a report. Three flags hide it until a moderator has a look.
